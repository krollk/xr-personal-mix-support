Kordeus LTD

XR Personal Mix

Personal monitor control for compatible Behringer X AIR and X32 mixers.

Support

Email support@kordeus.co.uk. Include your app version, Android version, mixer model and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Getting connected

Connect your Android device to the mixer's local network, open Settings, and find the mixer or enter its IP address. Select the unlinked mono Aux or bus configured for your monitor. Your engineer must set the routing. Demo mode is available without hardware.

The app does not stream audio. FX returns, stereo-linked outputs, WING and Midas consoles are outside the current supported scope.
